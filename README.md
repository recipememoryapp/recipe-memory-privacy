# Recipe Memory Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: September 24, 2026**

Recipe Memory is a personal recipe organization application that allows users to save, organize, and manage recipes and recipe links. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Recipe Memory.

## Information We Collect

Recipe Memory may collect the following information:

### Account Information
We may collect your email address and a user identifier when you create or use an account. This information is used to authenticate your account and provide app functionality.

### User Content
Recipe Memory stores content that you choose to save in the app, such as recipe links, recipes, collections, ratings, notes, cooking history, and other information you add to your personal recipe library.

Recipe Memory may also process content or links that you choose to import from third-party websites or services in order to provide recipe-saving and organization features.

## How We Use Information

Information collected by Recipe Memory is used to:

- Authenticate users and maintain accounts
- Save and synchronize your personal recipe library
- Provide recipe organization and related app features
- Process recipe information that you choose to save or import
- Maintain the functionality and security of the app

## Data Linked to You

Account information, user content, and user identifiers may be associated with your Recipe Memory account so that your saved information can be retrieved when you use the app.

## Tracking and Advertising

Recipe Memory does not use your personal information or user content to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Recipe Memory does not use your information for third-party advertising.

## Third-Party Services

Recipe Memory may rely on service providers to operate features of the app, including services for authentication, data storage, hosting, and processing. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

Recipe Memory may also allow you to save or access links from third-party websites and services. Those third parties have their own privacy practices, and this Privacy Policy does not govern their services.

## Data Retention

Information associated with your account may be retained while your account is active and as necessary to provide Recipe Memory's features, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security.

## Your Choices

You may choose what recipe content you save in Recipe Memory. You may also edit or remove content from your personal recipe library through available app features.

## Children's Privacy

Recipe Memory is not specifically directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Recipe Memory develops or its data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
